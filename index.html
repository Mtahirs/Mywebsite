About

Hi, I'm Muhammad Tahir. I started out on engineering internships, including a stint at the Jebba hydropower plant, before money and markets pulled me in. Since then I've moved from Kaduna to Lagos, worked as a product manager, and now work in investment management.

I write to think out loud: about money, markets, Islamic finance, and the questions that keep catching my attention. Some of it is practical, like how to buy your first stock on the NGX. Some of it is just me working something out in public.

Away from the numbers, you'll find me at a table tennis table, lost in a good book, or wandering through an art gallery.

Now
  • Studying for CFA Level 1, sitting the exam in May 2027.
  • Writing on Substack about money, markets, and life.
  • Slowly building something of my own.
